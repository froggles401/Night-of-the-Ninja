        How to play

        The goal

        Two Houses, Lotus and Crane, fight in secret. You only know your own House card. Its rank (1 is highest) shows how important you are to your House. With an odd number of players, one person is the Ronin and belongs to no House.

        A round

        1. House cards: everyone is secretly dealt one.
        2. Ninja draft: you get 3 Ninja cards. Keep 1 and pass 2 to the left, then keep 1 more and discard the last.
        3. The Night: five phases, in this order: Spy → Mystic → Trickster → Blind Assassin → Shinobi. In each phase everyone is asked whether to play cards of that type, so passing gives nothing away. Played cards resolve from the lowest number to the highest. A card you skip can't be played later in the round.
        4. Dawn: everyone reveals. The House with the highest-ranked survivor wins the round. If both Houses' best survivors tie, compare the next ones. A surviving Mastermind wins the round for their House outright.

        Turn timer

        Each decision has a countdown (⏱). If it runs out, a default move is made for you: you pass, answer No, or a choice is made at random when one is required. The host sets the length in the lobby, or turns it off.

        Dying and reacts

        Killed players stop playing cards but can still talk, bluff and advise. Mirror Monk and Martyr are reacts: you reveal one when a Shinobi or Blind Assassin chooses to kill you. Reacts don't work against the Judge.

        Winning

        Each round is a whole game: everyone in the winning House wins, even the dead, plus the Ronin if they survive. In a perfect tie, every survivor wins. The Martyr, Soul Merchant and Thief aren't used, so the game seats up to 10 players.

        Honor mode (optional)

        The host can turn this on in the lobby to play the full game over several rounds. It adds the Martyr, Soul Merchant and Thief and seats up to 11 players.

        • Everyone in the winning House draws an Honor token (worth 2, 3 or 4), even the dead.
        • A surviving Ronin draws one too. In a perfect tie, every survivor draws one.
        • Token values are secret. The first player to 10 points wins the game.
